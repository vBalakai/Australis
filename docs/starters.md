# Australis starters

Three partners, each with a three-stage line. The first two stages are a single type. The final form adds a second type that covers the line's weakness, in the style of the Gen 4 starters. See them as turning 3D models on the [starter gallery](../starters.html).

Each design is written once in `js/mon-designs.js` and built two ways: a simple overworld model that follows you, and a detailed battle model (faceted or smooth, with cel shading and ink outlines). Preview the battle models on the [battle page](../battle.html).

## Abilities

- **Gumjo line: Gum Bellow.** A sound attack that can make foes flinch.
- **Kindrill line: Frill Flare.** Lowers the foe's Attack when it enters battle.
- **Puggly line: Electrosense.** Its moves never miss.

## In the game

- Choose your starter from the capsules in the Professor's lab in Wattle Town. You can't leave town for Route 1 until you have one.
- Your rival picks the starter that beats yours: Kindrill if you take Gumjo, Puggly if you take Kindrill, and Gumjo if you take Puggly.
- Your partner follows you everywhere. Face it and press A to see its name, type and description.
- Gym battle scenes call your partner by name.
- Your partner evolves into its second stage after you win the Reef Badge in Coralport.
- Sugrette, rescued at the Bloomridge lookout, joins your team but your starter stays as the one who follows you.

## Balance check

Pyragon's Electric type does nothing to Koalossus, and Koalossus's Ground type also hits Platyrant hard. Platyrant may need an Ice coverage move.
