## Legendaries (#144–150)

Four guardian serpents each hold one colour of the rainbow; together they let the player reach the great serpent.

| # | Pokémon | Type | Concept | Found at |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 144 | Drouthorn | Fire/Ground | Drought and bushfire; cover of *Ember* | Ancient site, mid-game crisis |
| 145 | Monsurge | Water/Electric | Monsoon and cyclone; cover of *Monsoon* | Ancient site, mid-game crisis |
| 146 | Ochrasp | Fire/Rock | Red serpent of ochre earth; inspired by the red-bellied black snake | Opal Hollow, Red Centre |
| 147 | Aurasp | Electric/Psychic | Gold serpent whose scales flash like sheet lightning over the Top End | Sea cliffs near Stormhaven |
| 148 | Verdasp | Grass/Dragon | Green serpent of the ancient rainforest; inspired by the green tree python | Deep in Canopy Forest |
| 149 | Azurasp | Water/Ice | Blue serpent of the Southern Ocean | Sea caves near Frostport |
| 150 | Prismasp (working name) | Dragon/Fairy | The great serpent that balances the seasons | The Heartland |

**How the serpents work**

- Each guardian appears after its region's Team Snare operation is shut down, so the hunt runs alongside the main story.
- The player must catch or earn the trust of all four. Each one grants a coloured scale.
- In the Heartland, the four scales light the serpent's resting place. Only then can the player reach the wounded Prismasp and find the Ministry tag.
- Prismasp's ability, **Prismatic**, changes its type to match whichever guardian serpent is in the party when it enters battle.

Prismasp is a working name for an original serpent, keeping the earlier choice not to use the sacred Rainbow Serpent directly.
